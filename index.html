A little context

I care about the layer most people never see.

Right now I'm building an AI-first data platform for engineering and product at Adobe Express: ML inferencing APIs that personalize what people see, and semantic search that lets data scientists and PMs query data dictionaries in plain English instead of memorizing table names.

Most of it lives in the layer underneath: SDKs and client libraries that keep data collection consistent, and validation tooling that scans a million-plus TypeScript files to block bad analytics before they merge.

You'll still find me ramble about IaC, monoliths, and the occasional CI/CD rabbit hole, always chasing “speed-of-light” engineering: fast iteration, high ownership, and pragmatic calls under real production constraints.
